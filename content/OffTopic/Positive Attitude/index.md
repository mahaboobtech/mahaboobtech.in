## Does the trick work?
Here, the most important thing is mental health. If you are sad and lack confidence to do it, then you can't do anything unless you are in a good mood and able to make a small joke and able to take everything positively. Then, gradually, you start moving things easier. At least you won't fall and drop things there. If you weren't starting to move by making jokes, at least it makes you stand. Trust me, if you are able to stand, that is the step towards something great.

> Always remember, if you think you can, you can wage war with the world.
> If you can't do anything, then even your inner feelings will destroy you.

Why do you think people often paste some quotations in workplaces? Because people are meant to think negatively.

So, that's why I just made a trick: if you make jokes, then some happiness may occur, and that happiness will make you stand. Eventually, you start thinking positively, so making jokes is the key to thinking positively.

Once you get positive, you know the rest you can drive easily.

## Example
### Example 1:
You're studying something that you don't know, and it's boring. For example, you're studying Newton's laws of gravity. There are 3 laws.
Just try to think differently for a scene like:
- Why did the MF propose 3 laws? Why not one, so that it's easy to read and remember?
or
- On Newton's first law, joke: A body at rest will continue to be at rest until the wife notices and finds some work for him.

You're working in a company, and your boss is not giving you a holiday. Just make a joke like: Apparently, my desk and I are in a committed relationship now—no holidays allowed.

## Note
My article is supposed to make you think positively and make a better life, but joking unnecessarily may lead to some threats, so it should be like a spike, not every time.

And making jokes won't always be perfect. Sometimes you need other factors like interest, passion, removing all bad things, and keeping yourself in a good environment with good people. All these will make you think positively.

The most important thing is if you have confidence, then you don't need to make jokes because confidence itself is positive thinking. And don't be overconfident; be humble.

And I don't promote joking about other persons and their characteristics. It's strictly prohibited, and never ever try it; eventually, you will create your own demon.
