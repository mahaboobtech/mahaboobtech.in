# Here I am with another pep talk!
Hi friends, I'm here to share some of my thoughts about life. Some people won't connect, or don't accept me, or disrespect me, blah blah blah. To be honest, my feelings are: **I don't care**. I'm just giving an honest perspective view of my life, so if you don't like my view, just go away.

So, the first question in my life:
## Why are we here on this planet, and what are we doing here?
In my mind, nobody knows this answer. But from a spiritual view, it's like some kind of story to live happily, that God created us and we live here. First of all, I'm an Atheist, but I still read spiritual books, visit all holy places, and absorb their knowledge. I just gain the knowledge, and that's it.

> So, there is no meaning in life to live.

But what I know is to live happily.

## How to live happily?
Well, doing the thing you like, isn't that simple? But no, here so many real catches and factors come in.
The world is so cruel, especially in the 21st century. We witnessed all kinds of bad things and also some good things too.
No matter what you do, there will always be good and bad, appreciation and criticism both.

Don't ever, ever make your dependence on happiness via others:
Some people may say donating or helping others would give some happiness and also some virtue. Yes, it is. This should be done when you're strong enough.

So, as long as it's legal and ethical, without disturbing others, living peacefully by doing what you love to do is life.

**Don't ask me where I should find what I love.**

Just rewind your life once, see the things you wanted, just work for it and own it.

No matter how deep you fall, you still have to do what you like. But don't invest in others by sacrificing yourself. I have done so many times, but in the end, I got only disappointment.

I still don't know why mom and dad help us. No matter what we do, some parents will always support, and they don't expect anything from kids. I am lucky to have such parents.

So, except for parents, everything is like trading for me.

So, don't be like a money machine. Be what you wanted to be for goodness sake. If you want to know what life is, I recommend you watch *Sweet November*.

Well, there are quite a few topics to talk about. Not everyone will be like-minded, but commonly, some factors you need to be consistent in your mind:
- Believe in yourself.
- Be as perfect as you can.
- No matter if even God says you're wrong, don't care. Be what you are.
- Never be shy to acknowledge your mistake, because no one is perfect. Being imperfect actually makes humanity and differs from a machine.
- Never compromise, never settle.
- Be strong internally and externally.

So folks, I think you got my point of view why I always say the word "love".
